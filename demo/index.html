I

A structurally cheaper carrier in a $173B market that is finally underwriting at a profit.

U.S. homeowners direct-written premium reached $173.1B in 2024, up 13% year-over-year from $153B (Insurance Journal, Jun 2025). The incumbents carry a 25–30% expense ratio on legacy systems and headcount. An AI-native build runs at an 8% expense ratio — autonomous agents replace 60–70% of traditional insurer staff and cut operating cost by roughly 52.7% (Self-Insure 10-Year Proforma v03, May 2026). The return-of-premium product layered on top reframes the policy itself: stay claims-free, and a defined share of premium comes back.

IV

The downside is structurally bounded.

Three features compress the loss case before any operating performance is considered.

Investor Gate

A de-facto clawback at Month 24

A tri-party trust holds ~$90M. Miss the Month-24 milestones — 500+ policies, $1M+ GWP, loss ratio ≤60%, RBC ≥300% — and the trustee returns ~$55–70M to investors.

Capital Recovery

83% recover capital by Year 7

The Monte Carlo puts an 83% probability on full $100M recovery by Year 7, 91% positive at Year 10, and only 4.2% regulatory-insolvency risk (RBC<200%).

Low-Cat Launch

Ohio & Wisconsin first

Launch states chosen for low catastrophe exposure — OH combined ratio ~88%, WI ~91%, against a U.S. average near 99%. The model still clears a 1-in-25 cat year at ~+$298M / 3.0×.
